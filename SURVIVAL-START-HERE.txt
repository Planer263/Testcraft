HEARTH & COMPANY — SURVIVAL ALPHA

Extract the whole ZIP and open Wildblock.html. Existing graphics, models,
3D inventory icons and Chromebook options are retained. New worlds are Survival.
Use Survivors / Co-op on the title screen to choose a named survivor.
Each survivor has separate inventory, equipment, health, hunger, XP and bed
spawn. World chests, barrels and furnaces are shared. Local survivor switching
is implemented; networking is under construction in this intermediate package.
Export backups: the host/local world save stores the survivor records.
Not a complete implementation of the entire proposed roadmap.
